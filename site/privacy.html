CrewZero 私隱政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 12 日

CrewZero（零號隊員）由 Adam Chan 製作，是在你電腦上運行的開源程式。本政策說明 CrewZero 怎樣處理資料。

資料存放在你的電腦

  • CrewZero 沒有伺服器、帳戶或使用統計。
  • 你的公司資料、業績紀錄、名單、附件及延伸功能資料，全部存放在你電腦上、你指定的生意資料夾內（crewzero/data/ 及「延伸功能」資料夾）。
  • 資料保留多久、何時刪除，由你決定。
  • 除非你自行填寫意見表格，開發者不會收到你的任何資料。

會離開你電腦的資料

  • AI 工具：Zero 的工作由你已安裝並登入的 Claude Code、Codex 或 Antigravity 完成。
    • 工作所需的內容，例如你交給 Zero 的文字及附件，會經該工具送到其服務供應商，即 Anthropic、OpenAI 或 Google，按該工具的條款及私隱政策處理。
    • CrewZero 啟動該工具前，會移除名稱含 TOKEN、KEY、SECRET、PASSWORD、CREDENTIAL、BEARER、OAUTH 或以 CLAUDE 開頭的環境變數。
  • 上網研究：AI 工具可按工作需要搜尋及開啟網頁。
  • Google 文件連結：你貼上 Google 文件、試算表或簡報連結時，CrewZero 從 docs.google.com 下載匯出副本，存在你的電腦。
  • 連結檢查：Zero 引用的外部網址，CrewZero 會以 HTTPS 試開一次，確認能否開啟，不讀取或儲存網頁內容。
  • 網絡測試：認證 AI 工具時，CrewZero 連線 https://example.com/，確認電腦能上網。
  • 手機配對：只在你開啟手機配對後，才在家中或辦公室網絡開放給已配對的裝置使用。

意見及支持連結

頁面上的「給意見」（Google 表格）及「支持 CrewZero」（Buy Me a Coffee）只是連結。你按下並填寫後，資料由該服務按其私隱政策處理。

18 歲以下人士

CrewZero 為成年創業者而設，不以 18 歲以下人士為對象。

聯絡

CrewZero Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

CrewZero is open-source software made by Adam Chan that runs on your own computer. This policy explains how CrewZero handles data.

Your data stays on your computer

  • CrewZero has no server, no accounts and no analytics.
  • Your company data, results records, people lists, attachments and extension data are stored only in the business folder you choose on your computer (crewzero/data/ and the extensions folder).
  • You decide how long to keep the data and when to delete it.
  • The developer receives none of your data unless you choose to fill in the feedback form.

Data that leaves your computer

  • AI tools: Zero's work is done by the Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity tool that you have installed and signed in to.
    • The content needed for that work, such as the text and attachments you give Zero, is sent through that tool to its provider (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) and handled under that tool's terms and privacy policy.
    • Before starting the tool, CrewZero removes environment variables whose names contain TOKEN, KEY, SECRET, PASSWORD, CREDENTIAL, BEARER or OAUTH, or start with CLAUDE.
  • Web research: the AI tool may search and read web pages as the work requires.
  • Google Docs links: when you paste a Google Docs, Sheets or Slides link, CrewZero downloads an export copy from docs.google.com and stores it on your computer.
  • Link checks: CrewZero opens each external link that Zero cites once over HTTPS to check that it works, without reading or storing the page.
  • Connection test: while certifying an AI tool, CrewZero connects to https://example.com/ to confirm that the computer is online.
  • Phone pairing: only after you turn on phone pairing does CrewZero accept connections from paired devices on your home or office network.

Feedback and support links

The "Give feedback" (Google Form) and "Support CrewZero" (Buy Me a Coffee) items are links only. If you open and fill them in, those services handle your data under their own privacy policies.

People under 18

CrewZero is made for adult founders and is not directed at people under 18.

Contact

For questions or privacy concerns, open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/Adamchanadam/crewzero/issues
